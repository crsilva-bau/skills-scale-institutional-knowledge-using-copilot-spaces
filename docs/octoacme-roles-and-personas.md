# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness across teams and ensure deployments are planned, communicated, and executed safely.

### Responsibilities
- Define release scope, timing, and release criteria with Product and Project Managers
- Coordinate deployment readiness checks with Developers and QA/Testing
- Maintain release checklists, rollout plans, and rollback strategies
- Communicate release windows, impacts, and outcomes to stakeholders

### Key Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** align on code freeze expectations, deployment sequencing, and production support plans
- **Product Managers:** confirm release priorities, scope changes, and customer impact messaging
- **Project Managers:** synchronize timelines, dependencies, and go/no-go milestones
- **QA/Testing:** verify validation status and defect risk before release approval
- **Stakeholders:** provide release schedules, status updates, and post-release summaries

### How this role improves project outcomes
- Reduces release risk through consistent readiness and rollback planning
- Improves predictability of delivery dates and deployment quality
- Strengthens stakeholder confidence through clear release communication

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and coordinate test strategy so teams can validate quality early and consistently throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage goals, and quality gates
- Align acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers and Developers
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and release validation
- Track defect trends and drive root-cause follow-up with delivery teams

### Key Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** clarify testability expectations, review defect quality, and prioritize fixes
- **Product Managers:** ensure acceptance criteria are testable and aligned to expected outcomes
- **Project Managers:** integrate test milestones and quality risks into project plans
- **QA/Testing:** coordinate execution, reporting, and handoffs across test activities
- **Stakeholders:** communicate product quality posture and readiness signals

### How this role improves project outcomes
- Improves release quality through earlier and more structured validation
- Reduces escaped defects by enforcing clear quality gates
- Provides clearer quality visibility for planning and stakeholder decisions

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage communication flow between delivery teams and stakeholders to keep expectations aligned and decisions informed.

### Responsibilities
- Consolidate stakeholder inputs, constraints, and feedback across workstreams
- Translate stakeholder priorities into actionable updates for Product and Project Managers
- Coordinate regular status messaging, decision requests, and follow-ups
- Surface alignment gaps and communication risks early

### Key Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** route context on stakeholder expectations that affect implementation trade-offs
- **Product Managers:** align feedback synthesis with roadmap and prioritization decisions
- **Project Managers:** coordinate timing, cadence, and owners for project communications
- **QA/Testing:** communicate stakeholder quality concerns and validation expectations
- **Stakeholders:** maintain a clear single point of contact for updates and escalations

### How this role improves project outcomes
- Reduces confusion by creating a consistent communication channel
- Improves decision speed by packaging feedback and dependencies clearly
- Increases stakeholder trust through predictable, transparent updates

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
Risk Owners maintain accountability for high-priority project risks, ensuring mitigations are tracked and escalations happen before issues become blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Own identified risk items and keep mitigation plans current
- Define trigger conditions, response owners, and escalation paths
- Monitor risk indicators and report status changes in planning forums
- Coordinate contingency actions when risk thresholds are exceeded

### Key Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** identify technical risks and validate mitigation feasibility
- **Product Managers:** evaluate product impact and prioritization trade-offs from active risks
- **Project Managers:** integrate risk actions into plans, timelines, and status reporting
- **QA/Testing:** assess quality-related risks and mitigation effectiveness
- **Stakeholders:** escalate material risks and mitigation decisions with clear impact summaries

### How this role improves project outcomes
- Improves accountability by assigning clear ownership for critical risks
- Reduces schedule and scope disruption through proactive mitigation tracking
- Enables faster escalation and recovery when risk conditions change

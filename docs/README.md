# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This lightweight framework is designed to keep teams focused on customer value, deliver in small iterative increments, maintain clear ownership across roles, and continuously improve through reflection and learning.

## Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a practical lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** to define the problem, outcomes, and stakeholders.
2. **Planning** to break work into prioritized, shippable increments.
3. **Execution & Tracking** to deliver, measure progress, and manage blockers.
4. **Risk Management & Communication** to keep risks visible and stakeholders aligned.
5. **Release & Deployment** to ship safely with verification and rollback readiness.
6. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** to capture learnings and improve future delivery.

This approach emphasizes customer-first outcomes, iterative delivery, clear accountability, and a continuous learning culture.
